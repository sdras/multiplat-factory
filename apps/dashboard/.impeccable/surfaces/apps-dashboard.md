# Dashboard · release board

Scope: the dashboard's default presentation (apps/dashboard, `data-dashboard-style="board"`), Operate mode. Audience: the person who started a port, glancing back to see where it is, what it needs, and what it cost. Must stay untouched: the flight deck style, the pages' functions and copy register.

## Direction contract

THESIS: A product board, not an operations console: plain words, one task per card, decisions answered in place; the log stays behind a drawer.

OWN-WORLD: Light slate ground with white 14px-radius cards and hairline rules; one signal orange for actions and the current tab, a darker orange for accent text; status as lamp plus label. Dark theme: warm graphite, bone ink, brass accent. Familjen Grotesk throughout, 600 for titles. Hero art: the existing object renders, greyed and blended.

STORY: Where is my port, what does it need from me, what did it cost.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Top bar; board nav naming the project before its tabs, sun/moon toggle at the right; hero title with art; status row with actions; the decision card; platform progress with its steps beside budget and recent activity.

FORM: Round 2 V3 "Signal" (light) with V1 "Brass" (dark), a refinement of the incumbent board; round 1's roll (seed c3a458a9) was rejected by the user.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance.
